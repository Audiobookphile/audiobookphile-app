Audiobookphile is a modern, privacy-focused audiobook player built for seamless personal library streaming.

Features:
• Dynamic Liquid Glass interface with adaptive cover artwork theming
• Seamless 1-click server connection and auto-mapping
• Offline caching and playback queue management
• Variable speed playback and smart sleep timers
• Chapter navigation, bookmarking, and listening stats
• Multi-format audio support with hardware-accelerated playback

Stream from your self-hosted or cloud Audiobookphile instance anywhere.
